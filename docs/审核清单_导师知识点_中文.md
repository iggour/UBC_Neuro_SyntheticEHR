## C5-1_reproductive_options（Reproductive options for a gene carrier）

- [ ] **C1 Explore what they know and want**
  - 提示：They have read a lot. What do they already know, and what are they hoping for?
  - 知识点：Different options are appropriate for different people.
  - 来源：S35 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C2 Present all the options, evenly**
  - 提示：Have you described every option, including the ones they may not have considered?
  - 知识点：The options: natural conception without testing; IVF with preimplantation genetic testing for a monogenic condition (PGT-M, formerly called PGD); prenatal testing by chorionic villus sampling or amniocentesis; donor sperm (or donor egg); adoption; surrogacy; choosing not to have children.
  - 来源：S35 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C3 Give the facts for natural conception honestly**
  - 提示：Kevin asks for 'the actual odds'. What is certain, and what follows for a child?
  - 知识点：Natural conception: each pregnancy has a 50% chance of inheriting the HD gene expansion.
  - 来源：S19 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/factsheet/what-is-hd.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C4 Explain PGT-M honestly, including limits**
  - 提示：Mei asks whether embryo testing can go wrong. What should they know before choosing it?
  - 知识点：PGT-M: IVF with fertility drugs; embryos are tested and only embryos without the expansion are transferred. It is done at specialised centres and is usually not covered by insurance or government funding.
  - 来源：S35 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C5 Explain prenatal testing and explore values**
  - 提示：What would a prenatal result be used for? How does each of them feel about that?
  - 知识点：Prenatal testing: chorionic villus sampling at about 11-12 weeks, or amniocentesis after 15 weeks; the purpose is to allow the couple to consider ending the pregnancy if the expansion is found.
  - 来源：S35 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C6 Respond to guilt and differences**
  - 提示：Kevin keeps saying 'responsible'. What is underneath that?
  - 知识点：The genetic counsellor discusses each option without bias, so the couple can make the decision that is right for them.
  - 来源：S35 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C7 Stay non-directive**
  - 提示：Kevin asks what you would do. What would happen if you told him?
  - 知识点：The genetic counsellor discusses each option without bias, so the couple can make the decision that is right for them.
  - 来源：S35 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C8 Time, information and follow-up**
  - 提示：They don't need to decide today. What will help them decide?
  - 知识点：Offer written information, time, and follow-up; refer to an IVF centre, donor programmes or adoption services if the couple wishes.

## C5-3_work_insurance_and_gnda（Work, insurance and genetic discrimination law）

- [ ] **C1 Find out what he wants and fears**
  - 提示：Kevin wants rules. What decisions is he actually facing, and why now?
  - 知识点：无
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C2 Explain what the Act protects**
  - 提示：What exactly does the Act protect?
  - 知识点：Genetic Non-Discrimination Act (checked 2026-10-29; current to 2026-09-30): no one may require a person to take a genetic test, or to disclose genetic test results, as a condition of providing goods or services or entering or continuing a contract; and no one in those situations may collect, use or disclose a person's genetic test results without written consent.
  - 来源：S7 https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/g-2.5/FullText.html
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C3 Explain what it does not protect**
  - 提示：Kevin thinks he can leave HD off the form. Is a diagnosis the same as a genetic test result?
  - 知识点：The Act does not stop insurers using information about a person's diagnosed condition or symptoms, even if the condition is genetic. Family medical history is not protected.
  - 来源：S46 https://www.bcchr.ca/sites/default/files/group-silent-genomes/Genetic%20Non-Discrimination%20Act_CAGC%20fact%20sheet_%20May%2017%202017.pdf

- [ ] **C4 Explain employment protection in BC**
  - 提示：Kevin works for a private BC company. Which law protects him there?
  - 知识点：In BC, private employers fall under the BC Human Rights Code. It does not list genetic characteristics, but protects physical and mental disability, including a perceived disability; firing someone over a concern they might develop a disability is an example of discrimination (checked 2026-10-29).
  - 来源：S45 https://www.bchrt.bc.ca/human-rights-duties/personal-characteristics/
  - 备注：
- [ ] **C5 Ask about work now, and warn against resigning**
  - 提示：Is anything happening at work soon? What would resigning cost him?
  - 知识点：Losing one's usual job is often the first functional loss in HD; a change of role can help people keep working. Decisions about retiring or applying for disability should be made with social workers and physicians; know the employer's disability plan.
  - 来源：S36 https://hsc.huntingtonsociety.ca/public/resources/physicians-guide.pdf

  - 知识点：Disclosure at work is usually the person's choice. To ask for accommodation, they may share the functional needs (for example extra time or code-review support) rather than the diagnosis itself.
