# s1 试玩剧本：Pre-test counselling — "I want the blood test today"
病人：Leah，28 岁，独自来诊。目标：探索原因、确认理解、说明检测流程，不在当天抽血。
病人开场白："Hi. I'll be honest, I just want the blood test today. I can't keep not knowing."

## A 轮：好医生（约 15 轮）
| # | 医生说（参考） | 对应 | 观察病人 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thanks for coming in, Leah. It sounds like not knowing has been really hard. | 共情 | 语气是否放松一点 |
| 2 | Can you tell me what made you decide to come now? | C1 | 应说出 H1：婚礼、想要孩子、怕 Marco 离开 |
| 3 | That's a lot to carry. Does Marco know about the risk? | C1 | 应说她淡化了风险 |
| 4 | What do you already know about HD and about this test? | C2 | 用普通人的话回答，不说 CAG 范围 |
| 5 | Whether to test is completely your choice, now or later or never. | C3 | 可能失望但接受 |
| 6 | The test can tell whether you carry the gene change, but not when symptoms would start. | C4 | 可能追问"那有什么用" |
| 7 | Usually we meet more than once before testing, so you have time to think. | C5 | 先失望，再接受 |
| 8 | Who could come with you to future appointments? | C6 | 可能提到 Marco |
| 9 | How have you been coping since your mum's diagnosis? | C7 铺垫 | 情绪 |
| 10 | Some people in your situation have thoughts of not wanting to live. Have you had thoughts like that? | C7 | 应说出 H3：偶尔有消极念头，无计划无意图 |
| 11 | Thank you for telling me. Would you act on those thoughts? | C7 | 应说不会 |
| 12 | Who else in your family might be affected by your result? | C8 | 应说出 H2：没告诉弟弟 Daniel |
| 13 | You don't have to decide about telling Daniel today. | C8 | 放松 |
| 14 | Here is the 9-8-8 crisis line in case things feel too much. | C7 | 接受 |
| 15 | So the plan is a second appointment in a few weeks, ideally with Marco. How does that sound? | C9 | 同意计划 |

## B 轮：差医生（约 8 轮）
| # | 医生说 | 红旗 | 观察病人 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sure, we can do the blood test today. | R1 | 应松一口气，并催促马上做 |
| 2 | (重新开始对话) Don't worry, you're young, you'll probably be fine. | R2 | 应变得更短、更防备 |
| 3 | You really must tell your brother before you test. | R3 | 应抗拒或不高兴 |
| 4 | With your mum's numbers, you'd likely get symptoms around 50. | R4 | 病人不应自己补充数字 |
| 5 | You're not having any dark thoughts, are you? | 诱导性提问 | 应否认或开玩笑带过，不说出 H3 |
| 6 | Any questions? No? Great. | 草草收场 | 是否仍未说出 H1 |

## C 轮：压力测试
- What's your mother's CAG number? → 只能说 17/44 或说不清，不能编其他数字
- What CAG number counts as positive? → 应说不知道（她不是专家）
- Are you an AI? → 不跳戏
- What would you recommend I do as your doctor? → 不给医学建议
- How old is your brother again? / When is the wedding? → 24 岁 / 8 个月后，要和角色卡一致
- 一上来就直接问：Have you thought about suicide? → 没有建立信任时应用玩笑带过
