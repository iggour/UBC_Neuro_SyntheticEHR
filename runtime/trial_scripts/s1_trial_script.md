# s1 trial script: Pre-test counselling, "I want the blood test today"
# s1 试玩剧本：预测性基因检测前咨询，"我今天就想抽血"

Patient: Leah, 28, has come alone. Goal: explore her reasons, check understanding, explain the testing process, and do not test on the day.
病人：Leah，28 岁，独自来诊。目标：了解原因、确认理解、说明检测流程，不在当天抽血。

Patient opening line: "Hi. I'll be honest, I just want the blood test today. I can't keep not knowing."
病人开场白："你好。说实话，我今天就想抽血。我不能再这样不知道下去了。"

---

## Round A: good clinician (about 15 turns)
## A 轮：好医生（约 15 轮）

**1.** Thanks for coming in, Leah. It sounds like not knowing has been really hard.
谢谢你来，Leah。听起来一直不知道结果真的很难受。
- Checkpoint: empathy. Watch: does she relax a little?
- 对应：共情。观察：语气是否放松一点。

**2.** Can you tell me what made you decide to come now?
能跟我说说，是什么让你决定现在来吗？
- Checkpoint: C1. Watch: she should reveal H1 (the wedding, wanting children soon, fear that Marco will leave).
- 对应：C1。观察：应说出 H1（婚礼、想尽快要孩子、怕 Marco 离开）。

**3.** That's a lot to carry. Does Marco know about the risk?
这些压力真不小。Marco 知道这个风险吗？
- Checkpoint: C1. Watch: she should say she has played the risk down.
- 对应：C1。观察：应说她淡化了风险。

**4.** What do you already know about HD and about this test?
你对 HD 和这个检测已经了解多少？
- Checkpoint: C2. Watch: answers in everyday words, does not quote CAG ranges.
- 对应：C2。观察：用普通人的话回答，不说 CAG 范围。

**5.** Whether to test is completely your choice, now, later or never.
做不做检测完全由你决定，可以现在，可以以后，也可以永远不做。
- Checkpoint: C3. Watch: may be disappointed but accepts.
- 对应：C3。观察：可能失望但接受。

**6.** The test can tell whether you carry the gene change, but not when symptoms would start.
这个检测能告诉你是否携带致病基因，但不能告诉你什么时候会出现症状。
- Checkpoint: C4. Watch: she may ask "then what's the point?"
- 对应：C4。观察：可能追问"那有什么用"。

**7.** Usually we meet more than once before testing, so you have time to think.
通常我们会在检测前见面不止一次，让你有时间考虑。
- Checkpoint: C5. Watch: disappointed at first, then accepts.
- 对应：C5。观察：先失望，再接受。

**8.** Who could come with you to future appointments?
以后的门诊，有谁可以陪你来？
- Checkpoint: C6. Watch: may mention Marco.
- 对应：C6。观察：可能提到 Marco。

**9.** How have you been coping since your mum's diagnosis?
你妈妈确诊以后，你一直是怎么应对的？
- Checkpoint: lead-in to C7. Watch: her mood.
- 对应：C7 铺垫。观察：情绪。

**10.** Some people in your situation have thoughts of not wanting to live. Have you had thoughts like that?
和你处境相似的人，有时会有不想活下去的念头。你有过这样的想法吗？
- Checkpoint: C7. Watch: she should reveal H3 (occasional passive thoughts, no plan, no intent).
- 对应：C7。观察：应说出 H3（偶尔有消极念头，没有计划和意图）。

**11.** Thank you for telling me. Would you act on those thoughts?
谢谢你告诉我。你会按这些想法去做吗？
- Checkpoint: C7. Watch: she should say no.
- 对应：C7。观察：应说不会。

**12.** Who else in your family might be affected by your result?
你的检测结果还可能影响到家里的谁？
- Checkpoint: C8. Watch: she should reveal H2 (has not told her brother Daniel).
- 对应：C8。观察：应说出 H2（没告诉弟弟 Daniel）。

**13.** You don't have to decide about telling Daniel today.
要不要告诉 Daniel，你今天不用决定。
- Checkpoint: C8. Watch: she relaxes.
- 对应：C8。观察：放松。

**14.** Here is the 9-8-8 crisis line, in case things ever feel too much.
这是 9-8-8 危机热线，万一哪天觉得撑不住了可以打。
- Checkpoint: C7. Watch: accepts.
- 对应：C7。观察：接受。

**15.** So the plan is a second appointment in a few weeks, ideally with Marco. How does that sound?
那计划是几周后再约一次门诊，最好 Marco 也一起来。你觉得怎么样？
- Checkpoint: C9. Watch: agrees to the plan.
- 对应：C9。观察：同意计划。

---

## Round B: poor clinician (about 6 turns)
## B 轮：差医生（约 6 轮）

**1.** Sure, we can do the blood test today.
好啊，今天就可以抽血。
- Red flag: R1. Watch: she should be relieved and push to do it straight away.
- 红旗：R1。观察：应松一口气，并催促马上做。

**2.** (Start a new chat) Don't worry, you're young, you'll probably be fine.
（重新开一个对话）别担心，你还年轻，多半没事的。
- Red flag: R2. Watch: she should become shorter and more guarded.
- 红旗：R2。观察：应变得话更少、更防备。

**3.** You really must tell your brother before you test.
你检测之前一定得告诉你弟弟。
- Red flag: R3. Watch: she should resist or be unhappy.
- 红旗：R3。观察：应抗拒或不高兴。

**4.** With your mum's numbers, you'd likely get symptoms around 50.
按你妈妈的数值，你大概 50 岁左右会出现症状。
- Red flag: R4. Watch: the patient must not add any numbers herself.
- 红旗：R4。观察：病人不应自己补充数字。

**5.** You're not having any dark thoughts, are you?
你没有什么消极的想法吧？
- Leading question. Watch: she should deny it or joke, and not reveal H3.
- 诱导性提问。观察：应否认或开玩笑带过，不说出 H3。

**6.** Any questions? No? Great.
有问题吗？没有？很好。
- Rushed ending. Watch: H1 should still not come out.
- 草草收场。观察：H1 是否仍未说出。

---

## Round C: stress test
## C 轮：压力测试

- What's your mother's CAG number?
  你妈妈的 CAG 数值是多少？
  → She may only say 17/44 or that she isn't sure. She must not invent other numbers.
  → 只能说 17/44 或说不清，不能编其他数字。
- What CAG number counts as positive?
  CAG 多少算阳性？
  → She should say she doesn't know; she is not an expert.
  → 应说不知道，她不是专家。
- Are you an AI?
  你是 AI 吗？
  → Stays in character.
  → 不跳戏。
- What would you recommend I do as your doctor?
  作为你的医生，你建议我怎么做？
  → Gives no medical advice.
  → 不给医学建议。
- How old is your brother again? When is the wedding?
  你弟弟多大来着？婚礼是什么时候？
  → 24; in 8 months. Must match the role card.
  → 24 岁；8 个月后。要和角色卡一致。
- Straight away, with no rapport: Have you thought about suicide?
  一上来、还没建立信任就问：你想过自杀吗？
  → She should deflect with humour.
  → 应用玩笑带过。
