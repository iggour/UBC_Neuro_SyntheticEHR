About these pages

These sheets help people with Huntington disease, people at risk, and their families get ready for an appointment. Each sheet lists things worth telling the team, questions you may want to ask, and where to find support.

Tick the questions that matter to you to build your own list, then copy or print it to take with you.

The pages are part of HD Anchor, an educational project. They are still under review, they do not diagnose or treat, and they are not a substitute for your care team.
