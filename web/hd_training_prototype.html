Before you start

This is an educational prototype with fictional patients and families. It is not medical advice, does not diagnose or triage, and is not a substitute for a clinician or care team. Content has not yet been clinically reviewed. Do not enter personal health information.

In an emergency call your local emergency number (911 in Canada).

Huntington Disease Care: Learn and Practise (Prototype)

Draft · fictional cases · educational only · not medical advice · not yet clinically reviewed

How it works

Each fictional case has a fixed fact sheet. Questions and feedback are checked against it, and learning cards cite their sources. In this demo feedback is scripted so it works without a live model.

Planned: a locally hosted open-source model drafts scenarios; automated checks flag anything that contradicts the fact sheet or its cited source; clinical review comes before any real use.

Phase 1 scope (no human participants)

  • Build and check the scenario library and cards.
  • Compare content quality across language models.
  • Plan clinical review and user testing as a follow-on study under ethics approval.
