HD Anchor for Clinicians

Practising key conversations in Huntington disease care

All patients are fictional. AI-generated content for education only. Not medical advice.

For patients and families: go to the family pages.

Pending review. This website is still under review. Content and sources may change.

Why

Huntington disease is rare, so many clinicians meet few affected families. Some of the hardest parts of care are conversations: counselling before a genetic test, giving a positive result, and assessing suicide risk. HD Anchor lets clinicians practise these conversations before they happen for real.

How it works

  1. Learn. Short notes on the key facts, each linked to a published source.
  2. Practise. Talk with an AI-simulated patient who follows a fixed profile. An AI mentor can give hints, which you can switch off.
  3. Feedback. A checklist shows what you did well and what you missed, with links back to the notes.

The mentor only uses pre-written advice linked to sources. It does not write advice of its own.

Current stage

  • Fifteen scenarios across five fictional families are written and still under review.
  • Guided practice needs no AI. Free conversation runs on a model you choose, for example one on your own computer; HD Anchor does not see or store conversations. Our own tests use a locally hosted open-source model.
  • No human participants in this phase. Testing with trainees will follow ethics approval.

All people are fictional. No personal data is stored.
