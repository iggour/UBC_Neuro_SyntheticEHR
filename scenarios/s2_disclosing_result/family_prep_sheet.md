# Getting a predictive test result for Huntington disease
## Appointment preparation sheet (for people being tested and their companions)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-23. Reviewed by: project clinician (sources and content checked 2026-10-23)
Educational only. Not medical advice. Your genetics team is your main source of information.

### What may happen at the results appointment
- The result is given in person. You can still decide not to hear it, even on the day.
- The team will give the result clearly and then give you time. You do not have to ask anything straight away.
- You can ask for a written summary and another appointment for more questions.
- The team will contact you again within about a week, whatever the result.

### Tick the questions you want to ask (question builder)
- [ ] What exactly does my result show?
- [ ] Does this mean I am ill now?
- [ ] Can you tell me when symptoms might start? (Often this cannot be predicted.)
- [ ] What does my result mean for my brothers, sisters or children?
- [ ] Who should I tell, and do I have to tell anyone?
- [ ] What support is there in the next few days and weeks?
- [ ] When can we talk about plans for children?
- [ ] Is there research I could take part in later?

### For the person coming with them
- Your job today is to be there. You do not need to have answers.
- It can help to write down what the team says, so you can look at it together later.
- It is normal to feel shock, relief, anger or nothing at first, whatever the result.

### Support
- Huntington Society of Canada: https://www.huntingtonsociety.ca/
- If you or the person you are with have thoughts of not wanting to live: call or text 9-8-8 (Canada), or call 911 in an emergency.
