# Preparing for a family meeting about late-stage Huntington disease
## Appointment preparation sheet (for family members and carers)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-29. Reviewed by: PENDING (project clinician)
Educational only. Not medical advice. Draft, not yet clinically reviewed.

### Things worth thinking about before the meeting
- What did your family member say, at any time, about feeding tubes, hospital care or "machines"?
- What mattered most to them: being at home, being comfortable, being with family?
- Is there a representation agreement, advance directive or other written wishes? Bring a copy.
- How are you managing as a carer? Be honest with the team.

### Tick the questions you want to ask (question builder)
- [ ] Why is swallowing getting harder, and will it improve?
- [ ] What are the choices about eating and feeding? What can a tube do, and not do?
- [ ] What would comfort feeding look like?
- [ ] Who makes the decision if our family member cannot?
- [ ] What happens if there is another chest infection?
- [ ] Is it time to think about long-term care? How is it arranged?
- [ ] Can palliative care help us?
- [ ] Can family members who live far away join the meeting?

### At the meeting
- Ask for the meeting to include everyone who should be there, including by phone.
- It is fine to disagree. The team can help you think about what your family member would want.
- You do not have to carry this decision alone.

### Looking after yourselves
- Moving someone to long-term care when they need 24-hour care is a way of caring for them, not giving up.
- Ask about support for carers and for family members.
