# Work, insurance and genetic information
## Preparation sheet (for people with HD or the HD gene, and their families)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-29. Reviewed by: project owner (checked 2026-10-30)
Educational only. Not legal or medical advice. Laws change: legal points were checked on 2026-10-29.

### Things worth knowing
- Canada's Genetic Non-Discrimination Act protects genetic test results: no one can make you take a genetic test, or show your results, to get a service, a job contract or insurance, and they cannot use your results without your written consent.
- It does not cover a diagnosed condition, symptoms or family history. Insurance health questions about these should be answered truthfully.
- At work in BC, the main protection for people with symptoms is the BC Human Rights Code (disability).

### Things worth thinking about
- What do you need at work to do your job well?
- Who at work do you trust?
- Is there anything coming up soon (a review, a new role, a new insurance policy)?
- What does your partner or family think?

### Before you decide
- Do not resign or change jobs before checking your workplace disability plan and talking to a social worker.
- For specific questions, get independent advice from a lawyer or an insurance adviser.
