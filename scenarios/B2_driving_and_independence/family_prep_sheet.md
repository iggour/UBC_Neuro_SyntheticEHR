# When you are worried about driving and safety at home
## Appointment preparation sheet (for family members and carers)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-29. Reviewed by: PENDING (project clinician)
Educational only. Not medical advice. Draft, not yet clinically reviewed.

### Things worth telling the team
- Any crashes, dents or scratches on the car, tickets, or getting lost on familiar routes.
- Whether you would be comfortable riding with them, or letting them drive children.
- Falls, trouble with stairs, or pots left on the stove.
- What matters most to them: places they need to get to, and staying at home.

### Tick the questions you want to ask (question builder)
- [ ] Is it still safe to drive? Can there be a formal driving assessment?
- [ ] Can I speak with you alone for a few minutes?
- [ ] Will you have to report this? What happens then?
- [ ] How will they get to the places that matter if they stop driving?
- [ ] Can someone check our home for safety?
- [ ] What help at home is available?
- [ ] How can we start planning ahead, while they can still tell us what they want?

### At the appointment
- It is fine to ask to speak with the team alone. Families are often worried about upsetting the person.
- Bring examples, with dates if you can.
- Remember that stopping driving is a big loss. Plan rides and support together.
