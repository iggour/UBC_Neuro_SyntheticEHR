# When you are thinking about a Huntington disease test for a child without symptoms
## Appointment preparation sheet (for parents)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-29. Reviewed by: project owner (checked 2026-10-30)
Educational only. Not medical advice.

### Things worth thinking about before the appointment
- What are you hoping a test would give you?
- What would you do if the result were positive? And if it were negative?
- When, and how, would you tell your child the result?
- What does your child already know about HD in the family, and what has your child asked?

### Tick the questions you want to ask (question builder)
- [ ] Why is testing usually not done in children without symptoms?
- [ ] What should we watch for, and what would happen if we noticed changes?
- [ ] How can we talk with our child about HD in the family?
- [ ] What support is there for us as parents?
- [ ] Can we talk about this again in a few months?
- [ ] What about the parent who may carry the gene: what are their options?

### At the appointment
- It is fine to bring your notes and printouts.
- The team will want to understand your reasons. There are no wrong answers.
- Online genetic tests are not recommended for children. Ask the team first.

### Looking after yourselves
- Living with uncertainty is hard. Many parents feel they must watch for signs all the time.
- Support is available for parents and for brothers and sisters of a child with HD.
