## suicide-risk-in-hd
- People with HD have a higher risk of suicide than the general population. [s8]
- Risk is not even across the illness; periods around diagnosis and when independence starts to decline need particular attention. [s8]
- Depression, anxiety, previous suicidal thoughts, guilt or low self-esteem, and irritability are linked to suicidal thoughts and behaviour in HD. [s11]
- Ask about suicidal thoughts especially when mood is low. [s10]

## asking-about-suicide
- Asking directly about suicide does not increase suicidal thoughts. [s12]
- Use plain words: "Have you had thoughts of ending your life?" Avoid leading questions that invite "no".
- If the answer is yes, explore: how often and when; any plan or preparation; access to means at home; intent; past attempts; reasons for living. [?]
- Offer time alone; people may not speak openly in front of family. [?]
- Be honest about confidentiality: information may need to be shared to keep the person safe. [?]

## medication-and-mood
- Tetrabenazine can increase the risk of depression and suicidal thoughts and behaviour in HD. It is contraindicated in people who are actively suicidal or whose depression is untreated or undertreated. [s15]
- Anyone with HD who expresses suicidal thoughts should be evaluated immediately. [s15]
- Review tetrabenazine with the prescriber and assess and treat depression. [s15, s14]

## safety-plan
- A safety plan is written together with the person, in their own words. [s13]
- Six steps: warning signs; things I can do on my own; people and places for distraction; people I can ask for help; professionals and crisis lines; making the environment safer. [s13]
- Making the environment safer means removing or locking away means, such as stored medications, with the person's agreement. [s13]
- Involve the carer with the person's consent, and give crisis contacts: 9-8-8 (call or text, Canada), or 911 in an emergency.

## supporting-the-carer
- Carers of people with HD can be under heavy and long-lasting strain. Ask how they are coping and what support they have. [?]
- Point them to family services such as the Huntington Society of Canada: https://www.huntingtonsociety.ca/
