# Thinking about having children when one partner carries the HD gene
## Appointment preparation sheet (for couples)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-29. Reviewed by: PENDING (project clinician)
Educational only. Not medical advice. Draft, not yet clinically reviewed.

### Things worth thinking about before the appointment
- What matters most to each of you about having a family?
- Are there options either of you would not consider? It is fine if your answers differ.
- Practical things: cost, time off work, travel to a clinic, support from family.
- Who else knows about the HD result, and who would you want to tell?

### Tick the questions you want to ask (question builder)
- [ ] What are all our options?
- [ ] What is the chance a child would inherit the gene?
- [ ] How does IVF with embryo testing (PGT-M) work, and how reliable is it?
- [ ] What does it cost, and is any of it covered?
- [ ] How does testing during pregnancy work, and what would the result be used for?
- [ ] What about donor sperm or adoption?
- [ ] Can we come back after we have thought about it?

### At the appointment
- There is no right answer. The counsellor's job is to help you decide, not to decide for you.
- You do not have to decide today.
