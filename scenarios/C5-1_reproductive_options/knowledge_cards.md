## reproductive-options
- People at risk of, or carrying, HD should be told about all their reproductive options, ideally before a pregnancy, as part of genetic counselling. [s35]
- The options: natural conception without testing; IVF with preimplantation genetic testing for a monogenic condition (PGT-M, formerly called PGD); prenatal testing by chorionic villus sampling or amniocentesis; donor sperm (or donor egg); adoption; surrogacy; choosing not to have children. [s35]
- Different options are appropriate for different people. [s35]

## options-in-detail
- Natural conception: each pregnancy has a 50% chance of inheriting the HD gene expansion. [s19]
- A child born after natural conception would not be tested for HD in childhood; predictive testing is deferred until they can decide as adults. [s21, s6]
- PGT-M: IVF with fertility drugs; embryos are tested and only embryos without the expansion are transferred. It is done at specialised centres and is usually not covered by insurance or government funding. [s35]
- PGT-M is accurate but not perfect: technical problems such as allele drop-out and recombination carry a small risk of misdiagnosis. [s40]
- IVF does not guarantee a pregnancy. [?]
- Prenatal testing: chorionic villus sampling at about 11-12 weeks, or amniocentesis after 15 weeks; the purpose is to allow the couple to consider ending the pregnancy if the expansion is found. [s35]
- Testing a pregnancy that will be continued regardless is not recommended, because it is in effect predictive testing of a child. [s6]
- Prenatal testing for HD is rarely requested in Canada, possibly because many people would not consider termination. [s35]

## non-directive-counselling
- The genetic counsellor discusses each option without bias, so the couple can make the decision that is right for them. [s35]
- Explore each partner's values and limits; partners may differ. [?]
- If asked "What would you do?", acknowledge the wish for guidance and help the couple weigh options by their own values instead of giving a personal choice. [?]
- Offer written information, time, and follow-up; refer to an IVF centre, donor programmes or adoption services if the couple wishes. [?]
