# When HD starts: questions about treatment and research
## Appointment preparation sheet (for people with HD and their partners)
Drafted by: Claude (Anthropic), 2026-10-29. Reviewed by: PENDING (project clinician)
Educational only. Not medical advice. Draft, not yet clinically reviewed. Research news changes quickly; this sheet was checked on 2026-10-29.

### Things worth telling the team
- Changes you or others have noticed: movements, typing or handwriting, mistakes at work, mood, sleep.
- What you have read about treatments or trials, including anything offered for a fee.
- How you are both coping, including the partner.

### Tick the questions you want to ask (question builder)
- [ ] Has HD started? What did you see?
- [ ] What does this mean for work and daily life now?
- [ ] What treatments can help with symptoms?
- [ ] Are there clinical trials I could join? Would I get a placebo?
- [ ] What is Enroll-HD, and how do I join?
- [ ] Is a treatment I read about online safe or proven?
- [ ] What support is there for my partner?

### At the appointment
- It is fine to ask the team to write down what they found.
- Ask when the information about trials was last checked.
- Be cautious about clinics that charge for "treatments" outside a registered trial. Ask your team first.

### Support
