## what-is-juvenile-hd
- Juvenile-onset HD (JHD) means symptoms start at or before age 20. It is about 5 to 10% of all people with HD. [s16, s19]
- Early signs often differ from adult HD: stiffness, slowness and dystonia are common, and chorea may be mild or absent. [s16, s18]
- Falling school performance and changes in behaviour are common first signs. [s16, s18]
- Seizures are more common than in adult-onset HD. [s16, s17]
- JHD is usually linked to a larger CAG expansion. Over 60 repeats usually means juvenile onset, but about half of people with JHD have fewer than 60. [s16]
- There is no cure. Care aims to treat symptoms and support function, through a team: neurology, psychology or psychiatry, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, the family doctor and the dentist. [s16, s18]
- The course varies and cannot be predicted at diagnosis. [s18]

## giving-the-diagnosis
- SPIKES: Setting; Perception (what do they understand?); Invitation (how much do they want to know?); Knowledge (warning shot, then plain words, in small amounts); Emotions (respond with empathy); Strategy and summary. [s20]
- Give a short warning before the news, use plain words, and allow silence. [s20]
- A diagnosis can explain symptoms that have confused and frightened the child and family, and may relieve stress. [s18]
- Avoid implying that the child's behaviour was caused by poor parenting. [s16]
- Leave numbers (CAG repeats, life expectancy) out of the first conversation unless asked; check what they have taken in. [?]

## inheritance-and-the-family
- Each child of a parent who carries the HD gene expansion has a 50% chance of inheriting it. [s19]
- In JHD the gene usually comes from the father: about 70 to 80% of cases with onset between 11 and 20. [s16]
- A child can show symptoms before the parent. A grandparent's HD may have been missed or given another name. [s16, s18]
- Remarks about paternal inheritance need care, because families may become more worried about their other children. [s16]
- A parent's own predictive test follows the adult protocol and is their own choice. Offer genetic counselling. [s6]
- Predictive testing of a healthy brother or sister is generally deferred until they can decide for themselves. [s21, s6]

## telling-maya
- Parents sometimes do not want HD discussed in front of their child. This is a recognised difficulty in JHD care. [s16]
- An explanation for frightening symptoms can bring relief to the child as well as the family. [s18]
- Plan the conversation with the parents: who will tell her, when, where, and in what words; offer to be there. [?]
- Use honest, simple words, let her ask questions, and come back to it over several conversations. [?]
