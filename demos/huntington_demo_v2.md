# Addendum: Genetic Result and Treatment Decision (2026-09-09)

**HTT CAG repeat sizing (reported 2026-09-09):** alleles with 19 and 42 CAG repeats. The expanded allele of 42 repeats is in the full-penetrance range (40 or more), confirming Huntington disease. Reflex testing for other genetic choreas was not required.

**Assessment:** Genetically confirmed Huntington disease (42 CAG repeats), manifest, with chorea, oculomotor abnormality, mild caudate atrophy, and early behavioral and cognitive involvement. A paternal inheritance with a larger expansion in the affected generation is consistent with her reported father's onset in his late 40s to early 50s and her onset at about age 36. UHDRS Total Motor Score 28/124, TFC 11/13 (unchanged from 2026-08-19; not repeated today).

**Disclosure and counselling:** Result given in person with her husband present, supported by the genetic counsellor. She was upset but coping; no suicidal ideation on C-SSRS. Discussed 50% risk to each child, predictive testing eligibility for adult relatives only, reproductive options for future pregnancies (not currently planned), and that legal protections around genetic information exist but have limits, to be reviewed in detail with the genetic counsellor. She has decided to tell her sister herself, with counselling support offered.

**Treatment decision:** Started **tetrabenazine, titrated per standard schedule**: 12.5 mg once daily in the morning for the first week; increasing in the second week to 25 mg/day given as 12.5 mg twice daily; thereafter titrating upward at weekly intervals by 12.5 mg/day as tolerated, guided by chorea control and side effects. Doses of 37.5-50 mg/day will be given in three divided doses (maximum single dose 25 mg). If a dose above 50 mg/day is being considered, CYP2D6 genotyping will be obtained first. Review sooner for low mood, akathisia, sedation or parkinsonism; depression screening to be repeated at each visit. Referral to psychiatry expedited.

**Follow-up:** Clinic visit in 6 weeks (multidisciplinary HD clinic), with telehealth for interim reviews. Provided contact information for Huntington Society of Canada resources.

**Dr. Erik Lindqvist**, Neurology, Movement Disorders (fictitious)
