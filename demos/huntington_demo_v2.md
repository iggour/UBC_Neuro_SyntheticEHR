# Addendum: Genetic Result and Treatment Decision (2026-09-09)

**HTT CAG repeat sizing (reported 2026-09-09):** alleles with 19 and 42 CAG repeats. The expanded allele of 42 repeats is in the full-penetrance range (40 or more), confirming Huntington disease. Reflex testing for other genetic choreas was not required.

**Assessment:** Genetically confirmed Huntington disease (42 CAG repeats), manifest, with chorea, oculomotor abnormality, mild caudate atrophy, and early behavioral and cognitive involvement. A paternal inheritance with a larger expansion in the affected generation is consistent with her reported father's onset in his late 40s to early 50s and her onset at about age 36. UHDRS total motor score 28/124, TFC 11/13.

**Disclosure and counselling:** Result given in person with her husband present, supported by the genetic counsellor. She was upset but coping; no suicidal ideation on C-SSRS. Discussed 50% risk to each child, predictive testing eligibility for adult relatives only, reproductive options for future pregnancies (not currently planned), and legal protections and limits regarding insurance and employment. She has decided to tell her sister herself, with counselling support offered.

**Treatment decision:** Started **tetrabenazine 12.5 mg daily**, increasing by 12.5 mg weekly as tolerated; review sooner for low mood, akathisia, sedation or parkinsonism. Screening for depression to be repeated at each visit. Referral to psychiatry expedited.

**Follow-up:** Clinic visit in 6 weeks (multidisciplinary HD clinic), with telehealth for interim reviews. Provided contact information for Huntington Society of Canada resources.

**Dr. E. Lindqvist**, Neurology, Movement Disorders (fictitious)
